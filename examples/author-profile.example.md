# Author profile — example to customize

This is a fictional profile template. Replace its contents, keep your completed copy private, and explicitly ask the agent to use it. The skill does not load this file automatically.

## Context

- Audience: customers and colleagues.
- Typical work: email, product explanations, and occasional public comments.
- Languages: English and French.

## Voice

- Direct, calm, and specific.
- Explain the reason for a request when the reader needs it.
- Avoid promotional superlatives unless they have a clear basis.
- Keep deliberate humor in informal messages; avoid it in incident updates.

## Scoped conventions

- New customer emails: include a useful subject line.
- Short replies: avoid repeating context already clear from the thread.
- French correspondence: use the sign-off I supply for that conversation.
- Verbatim interviews: preserve wording unless I explicitly request an adaptation.

## Examples I approve

Add two or three short samples you wrote or may share. Explain what you like about each: its opening, rhythm, level of detail, or treatment of disagreement.

## Facts and experience

Supply any personal story or organizational practice needed for the current task separately. Tone preferences are not permission to invent a biography, client result, process, or promise.

## Changes

Record the context and reason for an important preference. Prefer “for customer incident emails” to “always” when the convention is specific to that situation.
