# External resource register

Access and visible terms checked 2026-09-19; these observations are version-specific. No third-party papers, benchmark rows or author writing samples are included in this package.

Public access, repository software licensing, dataset licensing and rights in underlying documents are different questions. An unverified license means this review did not establish reuse terms; it does not mean a resource is public domain or necessarily unavailable under all conditions. Recheck the exact resource/version before copying, training on or redistributing it.
